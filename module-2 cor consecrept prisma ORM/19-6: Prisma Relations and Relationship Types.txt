1. What Are Relations in Prisma?
In Prisma ORM, Relations define how two or more database models are connected.

Prisma supports three main relationship types:
1. One-to-One (1:1)
2. One-to-Many (1:N)
3. Many-to-Many (M:N)


2. One-to-One Relationship (1:1)
One record in a table is associated with only one record in another table.

Real-world example: One user has one profile.
