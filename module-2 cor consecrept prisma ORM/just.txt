
Most Common Interview Questions

Interviewers frequently pull from classic real-world systems, often categorized by their core technical challenges:
• Web & Utility Systems: Design a URL shortener (like TinyURL), a rate limiter, a web crawler, or a key-value store.
• Social & Messaging Apps: Design a real-time chat application (like WhatsApp), a social media news feed (like X or Instagram), or a notification system.
• Media & Storage Platforms: Design a video streaming service (like YouTube or Netflix) or a cloud file-storage system (like Google Drive or Dropbox).
